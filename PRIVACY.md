Privacy Policy for Study Lamp Focus Companion

1. Data Collection and Usage
The Study Lamp Focus Companion extension is designed to help users maintain focus. To achieve this, the extension requires the ability to read the URLs of your active browser tabs. This is strictly necessary to compare your current website against your locally defined whitelist.

2. Local Processing Only
All URL comparison and data processing happens entirely locally on your own machine.

3. No Data Sharing
We do not collect, store, transmit, or share your web history, analytics, or any personally identifiable information. No data is ever sent to external servers or third parties.

4. Companion App Connection
The extension communicates exclusively with your local Focus Timer desktop application via a local localhost connection ([http://127.0.0.1:54321](http://127.0.0.1:54321)).
